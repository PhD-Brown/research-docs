# <FontAwesomeIcon icon={faVial} /> Scientific Context

Welcome to the scientific section of the **Astro-Spectro** project.

This section will help you understand the scientific foundations that motivate the pipeline design. Going beyond the technical aspects, you will discover:
- The origin and characteristics of the spectroscopic data used,
- The methodology for processing spectra through to classification,
- How model performance is evaluated and outputs interpreted.

> Whether you are curious from an astrophysical perspective or want to deepen your understanding of the pipeline logic, this section provides all the keys.

<br />

:::info[<FontAwesomeIcon icon={faUserGraduate} /> Who is this for?]
This section is ideal if:
- You are new to computational astrophysics,
- You want to understand the origin and processing of LAMOST spectra,
- You are looking to connect model outputs to their scientific meaning.
:::
